Our Story

Education Beyond Classrooms

KEM High School is one of the leading State Board schools in Kanjurmarg East, Mumbai, dedicated to shaping young minds through innovation, leadership and academic excellence.

We focus on holistic student development through modern learning, coding, robotics, personality development and discipline-based education.
